# Emotional Garden

A mobile-first casual game built in Unity where your emotions shape the world around you. Players pick an avatar, choose how they're feeling, and step into a living garden that reflects their emotional state — complete with matching skies, music, and magical mini-games.

---

## How It Works

```
Start → Pick Avatar → Pick Emotion → Garden → Click Runes → Mini-Game → Back to Garden
```

1. **Choose your avatar** — Pick gender and skin tone (6 combinations)
2. **Choose your emotion** — Calm, Sad, Anxious, or Energetic
3. **Explore the garden** — Your avatar wanders and waters plants automatically, or you can tap anywhere to guide them
4. **Click runes** — Magical runes spawn periodically; clicking one launches a random mini-game
5. **Return and repeat** — After each mini-game, you're brought back to the garden

---

## Emotion System

Each emotion changes the garden's look and feel:

| Emotion | Sky | Music |
|---------|-----|-------|
| Calm | Blue sky with clouds | Peaceful looping track |
| Sad | Dark sky with black cloud | Melancholic track |
| Anxious | Stormy sky with gray cloud | Tense track |
| Energetic | Bright sky with sun | Upbeat track |

You can switch your emotion at any time through the settings menu.

---

## Mini-Games

Four mini-games that launch randomly from the garden:

- **Cloud Game** — Interact with clouds across the screen
- **Dark Game** — Navigate darkness to find light runes
- **Puzzle Game** — Drag shapes into matching sockets
- **Run Game** — Jump over incoming obstacles

---

## Features

- **Emotion-driven visuals & audio** — Sky backgrounds and music change based on your mood
- **Avatar wandering AI** — Your character idles and roams the garden on their own
- **Plant growth** — 9 plants with multiple growth stages, triggered by watering
- **Settings panel** — Volume controls for game sound and background music, plus a quick way to reselect your emotion
- **Mobile-first** — Built for portrait touch screens (1080×1920), works with mouse too
